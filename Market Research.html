Prepared by: Alec J. Holland | Rutgers Business School | Department of Finance

1. Market Overview

The New Brunswick residential real estate market remains anchored by high institutional demand, primarily driven by Rutgers University's student body and healthcare personnel tied to Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital. Due to tight inventory constraints in off-campus zones, property valuations and rental yield metrics remain resilient compared to broader Middlesex County averages.

4. Financial Assessment

Demand Stability: Low vacancy rates in student zones generate consistent gross rental revenue for real estate investors.

Operating Costs: Local property taxes require active revenue optimization—such as per-bedroom leases—to protect net operating income (NOI).
